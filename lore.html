The world

Pawtheon is reckoned in YFH, the years since the First Howl, and the tale is told around 1,200 YFH. Seven realms ring the neutral Starter Lands, where the Ancient Oak stands. Wisdom Owls and Crystal Lizards live in every realm.

The rules of magic

Hats are channels. A wizard's power flows through the hat, not from it.

Magic surges at the full moon and vanishes entirely at the new moon, so every Order lives by the turning of the sky.

The moons and the Orders

Each of six moons powers one Order, and each Order keeps its own spire. Together they hold 85 seats; the 86th belongs to the Wanderer, under the pale seventh moon.
