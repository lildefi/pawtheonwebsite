The Order

The Order of 86 is the 86 wizard-hat dogs within Doginal Dogs, a collection of pixel dogs inscribed on Dogecoin. Its members host and cohost X Spaces, day after day.

☾

The world

Our lore is set in Pawtheon, around 1,200 years after the First Howl: a land of seven moons and seven realms, with the Ancient Oak standing in the neutral Starter Lands at its centre.

✦

The magic

Hats are channels. Magic surges at the full moon and vanishes at the new moon. Each of the six Orders draws its power from its own moon, and the Wanderer walks under the seventh.
