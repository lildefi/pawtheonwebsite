Pawtheon

A browser adventure through the world of the Order: walk the seven realms, meet the 86 wizards, and follow the moons.

Start beneath the Ancient Oak

Pawtheon is planned as a game you play in your browser. You choose one of the 86 wizards, or a starter dog, and set out from the Starter Lands beneath the Ancient Oak.

What you'll do there

  • ⛭

    Explore the realms

    Discover landmarks across all seven realms and travel between them.

  • ★

    Meet the 86

    Find every wizard of the Order and hear their stories.

  • ◆

    Collect

    Search out Crystal Lizards and Moon Shards hidden across the world.

  • ❖

    Learn from the owls

    The Wisdom Owls teach lessons to those who seek them.

  • ▲

    Grow your Order

    Earn experience for each Order separately and collect achievement badges.

  • ✦

    Cast, fly and howl

    Use spells, take to the air, and raise your howl to the moons.

  • ♟

    Minigames

    Ideas include chess in Frosthollow, races, an arena, a crossword and a labyrinth.

  • ✉

    Play together

    Shared rooms with chat and emotes are planned for later.

Pawtheon is not playable yet. This page describes the concept only, and features may change.
